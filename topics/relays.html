Relays – Control, Timer & Overload

Control • Timer • Overload

1. Control Relay

Control relays switch control signals and can provide isolation or signal multiplication where required.

Applications

  • Interlocking
  • PLC interface
  • Contactor control
  • Breaker control
  • Alarm circuits

2. Timer Relay

A timer relay provides a time delay in a control circuit. Common functions include ON delay, OFF delay, interval and cyclic timing.

3. Overload Relay

An overload relay protects a motor against overload conditions and is commonly used with a contactor.
