TECHNICAL REFERENCE

Motor Starters

DOL • Star-Delta • Forward-Reverse

1. DOL Starter

DOL means Direct-On-Line. The motor is connected directly to the supply through switching and protection devices.

SUPPLY → MCCB/MCB → CONTACTOR → OVERLOAD → MOTOR

START energizes the contactor; STOP de-energizes it. An auxiliary contact may provide the holding function.

2. Star-Delta Starter

A star-delta starter is used with suitable motors to reduce starting current compared with DOL starting.

START
 ↓
MAIN + STAR ON
 ↓
TIMER
 ↓
STAR OFF
 ↓
TRANSFER DELAY
 ↓
DELTA ON

Star and delta contactors must be interlocked so they cannot be energized simultaneously.

3. Forward-Reverse Starter

Forward-reverse control changes the phase sequence to reverse motor rotation.

FORWARD ON → REVERSE CLOSE BLOCKED
REVERSE ON → FORWARD CLOSE BLOCKED

Electrical and mechanical interlocking should be provided as required by the design.
