TECHNICAL REFERENCE

Contactor

Power Switching Device

1. Introduction

A contactor is an electrically operated switching device used for switching electrical loads.

2. Applications

  • Motors
  • Pumps and fans
  • Compressors
  • Heaters
  • Lighting
  • Control systems

3. Main Parts

  • Main contacts
  • Auxiliary contacts
  • Coil
  • Electromagnetic core
  • Moving armature
  • Spring

4. Operation

When the coil receives its rated control voltage, the armature operates and the main contacts close. When the coil is de-energized, the spring returns the mechanism and the contacts open.

5. Auxiliary Contacts

NO, NC and COM contacts are commonly used for holding circuits, interlocking, indication, PLC feedback and control logic.
