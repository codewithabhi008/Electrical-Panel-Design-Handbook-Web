MCCB – Moulded Case Circuit Breaker

Moulded Case Circuit Breaker

1. Introduction

MCCB stands for Moulded Case Circuit Breaker. MCCBs are used for switching, isolation and protection of low-voltage circuits.

2. Applications

  • PCC and MCC Panels
  • Distribution Panels
  • Transformer Feeders
  • Generator Feeders
  • Motor Feeders
  • Changeover Systems

3. Types

  • Thermal-Magnetic
  • Electronic
  • Fixed-trip
  • Adjustable-trip
  • 2-pole, 3-pole and 4-pole

4. Protection

Depending on the model, protection can include overload, short-circuit, instantaneous, short-time and earth/ground-fault functions.

5. Accessories

  • Auxiliary contact
  • Alarm contact
  • Shunt trip
  • Under-voltage release
  • Motor operator
  • Rotary / extended rotary handle
  • Mechanical interlock

6. Breaking Capacity

Icu is ultimate short-circuit breaking capacity. Ics is service short-circuit breaking capacity. The selected value must suit the prospective short-circuit current.

7. Selection Factors

  • Load current
  • System voltage
  • Poles
  • Fault level
  • Breaking capacity
  • Cable rating
  • Ambient conditions
  • Coordination requirements
