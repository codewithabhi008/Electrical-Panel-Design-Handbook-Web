MCB – Miniature Circuit Breaker

Miniature Circuit Breaker

1. Introduction

MCB stands for Miniature Circuit Breaker. MCBs are commonly used for smaller electrical and auxiliary circuits.

2. Applications

  • Lighting
  • Socket circuits
  • Control circuits
  • Small motors
  • Auxiliary panel supplies

3. Configurations

  • Single Pole
  • Double Pole
  • Triple Pole
  • Four Pole

4. Tripping Characteristics

Common characteristics include B, C and D curves. The correct characteristic depends on the application and manufacturer's specification.

5. Selection

  • Rated current
  • Voltage
  • Number of poles
  • Breaking capacity
  • Tripping characteristic
  • Cable size
  • Load type
