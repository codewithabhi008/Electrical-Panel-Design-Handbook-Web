GA – General Arrangement

Panel Physical Layout

1. Introduction

GA stands for General Arrangement. A panel GA drawing shows the physical arrangement of components inside and outside the panel.

2. Typical Information

  • Panel dimensions
  • Compartments
  • ACB/MCCB locations
  • Busbar chamber
  • Cable alley
  • Metering area
  • Control devices
  • Doors and gland plates
  • Mounting plates
  • Foundation details

3. Design Considerations

  • Component dimensions
  • Cable bending space
  • Busbar clearance
  • Heat dissipation
  • Maintenance access
  • Door opening
  • Accessibility
  • IP requirements
  • Panel segregation
