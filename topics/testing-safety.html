TECHNICAL REFERENCE

Testing & Electrical Safety

Commissioning • Checks • Safe Work

1. Pre-Commissioning Checklist

  • Correct component installation
  • Correct breaker rating
  • Correct cable termination
  • Busbar connections
  • Terminal tightness
  • Earthing
  • Insulation condition
  • Control and auxiliary wiring
  • Protection settings
  • Interlocking
  • Indications and labels
  • Panel cleanliness

2. Functional Testing

  • Breaker ON/OFF
  • Trip operation
  • Emergency trip
  • Auxiliary and alarm contacts
  • Motor operator
  • Shunt trip
  • UV release
  • Changeover
  • Auto/manual modes
  • Interlocking
  • Indication lamps

3. Electrical Safety

  1. Isolate the electrical supply.
  2. Follow the approved isolation procedure.
  3. Apply Lockout/Tagout where required.
  4. Verify absence of voltage using an approved method.
  5. Use appropriate PPE.
  6. Prevent unintended remote or automatic operation.
  7. Follow manufacturer's instructions.
  8. Work only by qualified personnel.
Safety procedures must follow the site's approved procedure, applicable standards and manufacturer instructions.
