ATS – Automatic Transfer Switch

Automatic Transfer Switch

1. Introduction

ATS stands for Automatic Transfer Switch. It automatically transfers a load between two power sources.

2. Applications

  • Mains-DG systems
  • Mains-mains systems
  • Emergency power systems
  • Critical loads
  • Industrial and commercial facilities

3. Main Components

  • ATS controller
  • Source breakers
  • Motor operators
  • Auxiliary contacts
  • Control relays and timers
  • Electrical/mechanical interlocks
  • Indication and control devices

4. Source Monitoring

Depending on the controller: voltage, frequency, phase failure, phase sequence, under-voltage, over-voltage and source availability.

5. Typical Sequence

PREFERRED SOURCE HEALTHY
          ↓
PREFERRED BREAKER ON
          ↓
SOURCE FAILURE
          ↓
TRANSFER DELAY
          ↓
PREFERRED BREAKER OFF
          ↓
ALTERNATE SOURCE HEALTHY
          ↓
ALTERNATE BREAKER ON

6. Modes

  • AUTO
  • MANUAL
  • TEST, where supported
