ACB – Air Circuit Breaker

Air Circuit Breaker

1. Introduction

ACB stands for Air Circuit Breaker. It is mainly used for high-current low-voltage applications such as main incomers, bus couplers, generator incomers and large distribution feeders.

2. Applications

  • Main LT Panels
  • PCC / MDB Panels
  • Generator Panels
  • Transformer Panels
  • Bus Coupler Panels
  • AMF / ATS Systems

3. Main Functions

  • Switching and isolation
  • Overload protection
  • Short-circuit protection
  • Earth-fault protection where available
  • Remote and automatic operation
  • Interlocking and status feedback

4. Typical Construction

  • Main and arcing contacts
  • Arc chutes
  • Operating and trip mechanism
  • Electronic trip unit
  • Spring charging mechanism
  • Closing coil and shunt trip
  • Under-voltage release
  • Auxiliary and alarm contacts
  • Motor operator
  • Fixed or draw-out mechanism

5. Trip Functions

Common electronic protection functions are L (Long Time), S (Short Time), I (Instantaneous) and G (Ground/Earth Fault), commonly referred to as LSIG.

6. Draw-Out Positions

A draw-out ACB may have CONNECTED, TEST and DISCONNECTED positions. The exact operating meaning must be verified from the manufacturer.

7. Accessories

  • Closing coil
  • Shunt trip
  • Under-voltage release
  • Spring-charging motor
  • Auxiliary contacts
  • Alarm contacts
  • Mechanical/electrical interlock
  • Communication module
Engineering note: exact ratings, terminal numbers, accessories, dimensions, settings and control circuits must be verified from the selected manufacturer's documentation and approved project drawings.
